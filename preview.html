Warm hello to parents

My name is Thuy, an independent part-time nanny based in Ho Chi Minh City. I have more than 20 years of hands-on experience caring for infants, toddlers and young children from different cultural backgrounds.

I understand that every family has its own routine. My approach focuses on gentle patience, attentive safety, clear communication and helping children feel comfortable in their familiar environment.

Whether you need a few quiet hours to work, support during a trip, an evening babysitter or recurring part-time care, I aim to make the experience simple and reassuring for both parents and children.
