Frequently asked questions

Can you come directly to my hotel?

Yes. Care can be provided at hotels and serviced apartments in Ho Chi Minh City, subject to availability and the property’s visitor policy.

Do you speak English?

Yes. I communicate in English with international parents and have previous experience working abroad.

What ages do you care for?

I care for children from newborn age through infants, toddlers, young children, and older children.

Can I book for an evening?

Yes. Evening babysitting can be arranged depending on availability, with the session timing confirmed before the booking.

Which areas do you cover?

I provide childcare across Ho Chi Minh City, with frequent availability in central and eastern areas such as District 1, District 3, District 4, Binh Thanh, and Thu Duc.
