Warm Hello to Parents!

My name is Thuy, an independent part-time nanny based in Ho Chi Minh City. First and foremost, thank you for stopping by!.

I deeply understand that modern parenting requires balance, and every family's journey is unique. Whether you are a local parent needing a few quiet hours to focus on work, an expat family settling into Saigon, or travelers hoping to enjoy a peaceful dinner date, finding a trustworthy hand to care for your little ones is essential.

With over 20 years of dedicated hands-on experience caring for infants, toddlers, and young children from diverse cultural backgrounds, I view babysitting not merely as a task, but as a meaningful connection. My approach centers on gentle patience, clear communication, active listening, and creating a safe, cheerful space where children feel valued and comfortable—even when meeting someone new for the first time.

When you welcome me into your child's routine, you can rest assured knowing they are surrounded by warmth, attentive safety, and constructive engagement every single minute.
