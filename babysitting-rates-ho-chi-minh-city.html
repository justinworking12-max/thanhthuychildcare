What can affect the final rate?

Instead of using one fixed price for every family, the quote reflects the practical details of the session.

Schedule & duration

The requested date, start and finish time, total hours, and whether the session is during the day or evening.

Location

Your home, apartment, hotel, serviced apartment, and travel distance within Ho Chi Minh City.

Care requirements

Your child’s age, number of children, routines, and any additional care needs you would like to discuss.

What the starting rate means

“From 200,000 VND/hour” is the starting point for hourly care. It is not a promise that every booking will have the same final rate. This keeps pricing transparent while allowing the quote to reflect the actual schedule and care requested.

Frequently asked questions

Is 200,000 VND/hour the fixed price?

No. It is the starting rate. The final quote is confirmed after the booking details are shared.

Can I ask for the rate before I book?

Yes. Send the date, hours, location, child’s age, and care requirements to receive a confirmed rate before booking.

Do hotel bookings use the same starting rate?

Hotel and serviced-apartment bookings also start from 200,000 VND/hour, with the final rate based on the specific booking details.

Can I book only a few hours?

Yes. The service is designed around flexible hourly childcare, subject to availability.
